Sources & methodology Support

Built to explain the rate, not just show it.

QuietRate keeps the main conversion simple, while making the context behind a rate easy to inspect when you need it.

Convert at a glance

See the currencies you care about together. Change the input currency with a tap while keeping the equivalent value in context.

Understand the rate

Open Rate Details to view the current rate, inverse rate, historical movement, and useful period statistics without turning the experience into a trading terminal.

Know when the data applies

QuietRate distinguishes between a rate’s effective date and the time the data was retrieved, so those two concepts are never presented as the same thing.

See the source

Data provenance belongs in the product, not hidden in fine print. QuietRate provides a dedicated place for source, attribution, methodology, and reference-rate information.

Designed for iPhone

QuietRate is built natively for iOS, with Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, accessibility support, and a focused interface designed around quick everyday use.

Calm by design

No trading-style green and red signals, no speculative market cues, and no unnecessary dashboard noise. The interface stays focused on reference and conversion.

Reference information, not an executable rate.

QuietRate is designed for informational reference. Exchange rates shown by QuietRate may differ from rates offered by banks, card networks, money-transfer services, merchants, or other financial providers.
